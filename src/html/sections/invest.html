Why Invest in Dubai Maritime City?

Dubai Maritime City offers a unique blend of modern infrastructure, premium real estate, and a thriving business ecosystem, making it an attractive destination for investment and lifestyle opportunities.

  • High Rental Yields

    Residential properties in Dubai Maritime City (DMC) offer rental yields of 6–8% annually, higher than the Dubai average of 5.5%

  • Freehold Ownership

    Dubai Land Department guarantees secure property laws and 100% foreign ownership for all nationalities.

  • Strategic Location

    Situated between Port Rashid and the Drydocks World, with quick access to maritime hubs, Sheikh Zayed Road, and the city center.

  • Capital Appreciation

    Property values are expected to grow by 5–8% annually, driven by demand and the allure of luxury living.

  • Future Growth Potential

    Marina expansions and sustainability initiatives in DMC boost its appeal, attracting investors and increasing property value.

  • Taxes and Fees

    Dubai Land Department (DLD) fee is 4% of the property value, while the standard agency fee in Dubai is 2% of the property price.
