Why Invest in Dubai Maritime City?

Dubai Maritime City offers a unique blend of modern infrastructure, premium real estate, and a thriving business ecosystem, making it an attractive destination for investment and lifestyle opportunities.

  • High Rental Yields

    Residential properties in Dubai Maritime City (DMC) offer rental yields of 6–8% annually, higher than the Dubai average of 5.5%

  • Freehold Ownership

    Secure and transparent property laws under Dubai Land Department (DLD)

    Golden 100% foreign ownership for all nations

  • Strategic Location

    Situated between Port Rashid and the Drydocks World, with quick access to major maritime hubs

    Well-connected to Sheikh Zayed Road and the city center

  • Capital Appreciation

    Property values are expected to grow by 5–8% annually due to increased demand

    Luxury properties enhance the area's premium charm

  • Future Growth Potential

    Ongoing developments in DMC, including marina expansions, will enhance the area’s profile

    Sustainability initiatives attract eco-conscious investors, adding value to properties

  • Taxes and Fees

    Agency Fee 2% of the property price (standard across Dubai)

    Dubai Land Department (DLD) Fee 4% of the property value
